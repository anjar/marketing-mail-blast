/speckit-specify

Build a non-production bulk email campaign dry-run application that allows an operator to submit one personalized email campaign to a preloaded recipient dataset containing up to 1,000,000 or more records.

This feature is intended to validate correctness, resumability, bounded processing, progress reporting, and throughput using a non-production email target. It does not send email through a production email service.

## User Experience

The application has one simple campaign form with:

* a required email subject field;
* a required plain-text email body field;
* a Send button.

No authentication, login, account management, or user roles are required.

The recipient list is not entered or uploaded through this form. Recipients are provided separately as a preloaded test dataset.

When the operator submits the form, the system creates one campaign targeting a fixed snapshot of the recipient records available at the time the campaign submission is accepted.

Recipients added after that point are not included in the running campaign.

## Campaign Submission

The subject and body must contain at least one non-whitespace character.

Submitting a valid campaign starts processing without requiring a second confirmation step.

At most one campaign may be in a non-terminal state at any time.

While a campaign is active:

* the subject field, body field, and Send button must be disabled or hidden;
* the page must display a visible banner stating that a mail blast is currently processing;
* additional campaign creation requests must be rejected;
* opening another browser tab or calling the campaign creation interface directly must not bypass this restriction.

If multiple valid campaign requests arrive concurrently, exactly one request may create a campaign. Every other request must receive a deterministic conflict response identifying that another campaign is active.

Submitting the same request twice, including through a double-click or request retry, must not result in two active campaigns.

## Personalization

The campaign body may contain the exact placeholder:

`{{name}}`

For a recipient with a non-empty name, every occurrence of `{{name}}` is replaced with that recipient's name.

A name containing only whitespace is treated as missing.

For a recipient with an empty, missing, or whitespace-only name, `{{name}}` is replaced with:

`Customer`

If the campaign body does not contain `{{name}}`, the recipient name is ignored.

No other template variables or template language features are supported in this phase.

The email subject is not personalized in this phase.

## Recipient Validation

Each recipient email address must be validated for syntax before that recipient is submitted for delivery.

Validation checks email format only. The system must not claim that an address exists, accepts email, or has valid MX or DNS records.

Leading and trailing whitespace must be removed before validation.

Recipients with missing, empty, whitespace-only, or syntactically invalid email addresses must not be submitted for delivery.

Each rejected recipient must have an explicit rejection reason.

At minimum, rejection reasons must distinguish:

* `EMAIL_MISSING`
* `EMAIL_INVALID_FORMAT`
* `DUPLICATE_EMAIL`

## Duplicate Recipients

Within one campaign, email addresses are compared after trimming surrounding whitespace and converting the address to lowercase.

No normalized email address may be intentionally submitted for delivery more than once in the same campaign.

When multiple recipient records have the same normalized email address:

* the recipient record with the earliest stable recipient identifier is accepted;
* later matching records are rejected with reason `DUPLICATE_EMAIL`.

The accepted record supplies the name used for personalization.

## Recipient Outcomes

Every recipient in the campaign snapshot must eventually have one explicit disposition.

A recipient may be rejected before delivery or accepted for delivery.

Rejected recipients do not enter the delivery pipeline.

Every accepted recipient must eventually reach exactly one of these terminal delivery outcomes:

* `SENT`: the dry-run email target positively confirmed acceptance;
* `FAILED`: delivery definitively failed and a failure reason was recorded;
* `DELIVERY_UNKNOWN`: the system cannot determine whether the target accepted the email.

A recipient whose delivery is still waiting, queued, being processed, or awaiting retry remains non-terminal and is counted as pending.

A recipient marked `SENT`, `FAILED`, or `DELIVERY_UNKNOWN` must not be automatically submitted again after an application restart.

The system must never silently convert an uncertain delivery into either `SENT` or `FAILED`.

## Progress Reporting

The operator must be able to request current campaign progress while processing is still underway.

The page provides a Refresh button that requests the latest persisted progress.

Automatic polling, Server-Sent Events, WebSockets, and push notifications are not required in this phase.

Refreshing the browser page must not lose access to the active campaign's progress.

Progress must show:

* campaign status;
* total recipient records in the campaign snapshot;
* pending recipients;
* rejected recipients;
* sent recipients;
* failed recipients;
* unknown-delivery recipients;
* total delivery attempts;
* campaign start time;
* campaign completion time, when complete.

Rejected recipients must be broken down by rejection reason.

Failed recipients must be broken down by delivery failure reason.

At all times, the following reconciliation rule must hold:

`total recipients = pending + rejected + sent + failed + delivery unknown`

Counts must come from persisted campaign data rather than browser-local state.

Queue-observability information is not a substitute for campaign progress data.

## Campaign Completion

A campaign remains active while at least one recipient in its snapshot is pending.

A campaign reaches `COMPLETED` when:

* no recipients remain pending;
* all accepted recipients have reached terminal outcomes;
* no recipients have a `FAILED` or `DELIVERY_UNKNOWN` outcome.

A campaign reaches `COMPLETED_WITH_ISSUES` when:

* no recipients remain pending; and
* at least one recipient was rejected, failed, or ended with unknown delivery.

Once the campaign reaches a terminal status:

* the form becomes available again;
* the operator may submit a new campaign;
* the previous campaign's final progress remains queryable.

A campaign must not be reported as complete if its aggregate counts do not reconcile with its recipient records.

## Interruption and Recovery

The system must recover from interruption while a campaign is active.

After the application is restarted:

* the same active campaign must remain identifiable;
* persisted terminal recipient outcomes must remain unchanged;
* recipients already marked `SENT` must not be sent again;
* recipients marked `FAILED` must not be automatically retried;
* recipients marked `DELIVERY_UNKNOWN` must not be automatically retried;
* non-terminal recipient work may continue or be reconstructed;
* campaign counts must remain reconcilable.

The recovery process must not require the entire recipient dataset to be loaded into memory.

Duplicate execution of the same unit of work must not intentionally produce a second delivery for a recipient that already has a terminal outcome.

Manual campaign pause, cancellation, deletion, and restart-from-beginning features are not included in this phase.

## Large Dataset Behavior

The application must support campaign datasets containing at least 1,000,000 recipient records.

It must not require the complete recipient list, complete personalized message set, or complete delivery-job set to be held in application memory simultaneously.

Processing may take place incrementally, but the operator experiences it as one campaign.

Campaign submission and progress queries must remain usable while recipient processing is underway.

No fixed campaign completion-time guarantee is established for this phase. Throughput and duration must be measured and reported rather than assumed.

## User Scenarios and Acceptance Criteria

### User Story 1: Submit a Campaign

As an operator, I can enter a subject and body and submit one campaign so that the preloaded recipient dataset is processed.

Acceptance scenarios:

1. Given no campaign is active, when the operator submits a non-empty subject and body, then one campaign is accepted and processing begins.
2. Given the subject or body contains only whitespace, when the operator submits the form, then no campaign is created and validation errors are shown.
3. Given a campaign is active, when another campaign request is submitted, then the request is rejected without creating another campaign.
4. Given two campaign submissions arrive concurrently, when they are processed, then exactly one campaign is created.

### User Story 2: Personalize and Validate Recipients

As an operator, I need each valid recipient to receive the correct personalized dry-run message while invalid and duplicate records are explicitly rejected.

Acceptance scenarios:

1. Given a recipient named `Alicia` and a body containing `{{name}}`, when the message is generated, then each placeholder occurrence is replaced with `Alicia`.
2. Given a recipient with no usable name and a body containing `{{name}}`, when the message is generated, then the placeholder is replaced with `Customer`.
3. Given the body contains no `{{name}}` placeholder, when the message is generated, then the recipient name does not affect the body.
4. Given an invalid email address, when the recipient is validated, then it is rejected and no delivery attempt is created.
5. Given duplicate normalized email addresses, when the campaign is processed, then only the earliest stable recipient record is accepted and all later duplicates are rejected.

### User Story 3: View Campaign Progress

As an operator, I can refresh campaign progress while processing continues so that I can observe its current state.

Acceptance scenarios:

1. Given a campaign is active, when the operator clicks Refresh, then the latest persisted counts and status are displayed.
2. Given delivery failures have occurred, when progress is refreshed, then failed counts are grouped by reason.
3. Given recipient validation failures have occurred, when progress is refreshed, then rejected counts are grouped by reason.
4. Given the browser is reloaded, when the page opens again, then the current active campaign and its latest progress remain visible.
5. Given progress is displayed, then all recipient outcome counts reconcile with the campaign total.

### User Story 4: Recover an Interrupted Campaign

As an operator, I need an interrupted campaign to continue safely without resending recipients whose outcomes are already terminal.

Acceptance scenarios:

1. Given some recipients are marked `SENT`, when the application is restarted, then those recipients are not sent again.
2. Given some recipients are pending, when the application is restarted, then their work can continue without resetting completed outcomes.
3. Given the same work is executed more than once, when a terminal outcome already exists, then no new intended delivery is made for that recipient.
4. Given delivery acceptance became uncertain during interruption, when recovery occurs, then the recipient is recorded as `DELIVERY_UNKNOWN` rather than being silently marked sent, failed, or automatically retried.

### User Story 5: Finish and Start Another Campaign

As an operator, I can submit a new campaign after the active campaign has finished.

Acceptance scenarios:

1. Given no recipients remain pending, when all totals reconcile, then the campaign enters an appropriate terminal status.
2. Given the campaign reaches a terminal status, then the subject, body, and Send button become available.
3. Given a previous campaign is complete, when a new valid campaign is submitted, then the new campaign is accepted.
4. Given a campaign still has pending recipients, then the form remains unavailable.

## Success Criteria

1. A campaign containing 1,000,000 recipient records can be processed without loading the complete recipient set into application memory at once.
2. Every recipient in the campaign snapshot is represented in exactly one current disposition count.
3. When a campaign completes, zero recipients remain pending.
4. Campaign-level totals exactly reconcile with recipient-level records.
5. Invalid email addresses never produce delivery attempts.
6. No normalized email address is intentionally delivered more than once within one campaign.
7. Restart tests preserve terminal outcomes and do not resend recipients already marked `SENT`.
8. Progress remains queryable throughout campaign processing.
9. Concurrent submissions never create more than one active campaign.
10. Validation runs are performed incrementally with 1,000, 10,000, 100,000, and 1,000,000 recipients.
11. Each scale run records total duration, throughput, outcome counts, duplicate count, unknown-delivery count, and observed resource consumption.
12. A full-scale run is attempted only after all smaller validation runs complete without unresolved correctness defects.

## Explicit Non-Goals

This feature does not include:

* production email delivery;
* production email service-provider integration;
* authentication or authorization;
* recipient entry or CSV upload through the campaign page;
* editing the recipient dataset through the application;
* HTML or rich-text email editing;
* subject personalization;
* template variables other than `{{name}}`;
* automatic progress updates;
* campaign scheduling;
* concurrent campaigns;
* campaign pause or cancellation;
* manual retry of terminal recipients;
* A/B testing;
* open or click tracking;
* bounce or complaint handling;
* unsubscribe management;
* DNS, MX, mailbox-existence, or deliverability verification;
* production deployment requirements.
