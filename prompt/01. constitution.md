/speckit-constitution This project is a one-off bulk email campaign sender. It sends one personalized promotional email to up to 1,000,000+ recipients as fast as reasonably possible, using a non-production email target for testing.

Principles for this project:

1. No silent failures. 
  Every recipient must resolve to exactly one terminal, unambiguous outcome. A recipient that is neither confirmed sent nor confirmed failed (with a reason) is a bug, not an edge case.

2. Fail fast, fail cheap.
  Invalid input must be caught and recorded before any expensive or irreversible operation is attempted on it — never discovered downstream.

3. Safe to interrupt. Any long-running process must be resumable without causing duplicate side effects. Assume the process will be interrupted at some point; design for it rather than treating it as exceptional.

4. Measure before trusting. 
  Performance and throughput claims must be validated empirically at increasing scale before being relied upon at full scale. Do not assume a number that hasn't been observed.

5. Minimum sufficient scope. 
  Prefer the simplest solution that satisfies the stated requirements. Do not add integrations, abstractions, or features beyond what the specification calls for.

6. Traceable reasoning. 
  Every non-trivial technical decision must be justified in the plan it belongs to, not assumed silently by whichever phase happens to touch it first.


Non-goals (explicitly out of scope):
- No real ESP integration (SendGrid, SES, etc.)
- No authentication, multi-tenancy, or user accounts
- No scheduling, A/B testing, or multi-campaign management
- No MX/DNS-level email deliverability verification — format check only
